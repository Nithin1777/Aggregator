Every vendor files its products under its own shelf names — Robu calls them “MLCCs- Multi Layer Ceramic Capacitors”, QuartzComponents calls them “SMD Capacitors”, MakerBazar simply says “Capacitors”. Aggregate maps all of them onto one shared taxonomy, so a single filter means the same thing no matter which vendor a part came from.
